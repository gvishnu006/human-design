The five types

Your type describes how you move through the world and where your decision energy lives. It is the most visible part of a chart — and the least complete on its own.

Generator

The most common type. Your energy is built for sustained, meaningful work — the kind that produces a visible return.

Authority
Sacral — a body-level yes or no
Gift
Knowing what you actually want
Watch for
